About Us

Inequality Solver was created to help students and learners understand how to solve inequalities — not just get the answer, but truly understand every step along the way.

We believe math should be clear, friendly, and beautiful. That's why every solution shows a full step-by-step breakdown, proper interval notation with correct brackets, and an accurate number line so you can see exactly what the solution looks like.
